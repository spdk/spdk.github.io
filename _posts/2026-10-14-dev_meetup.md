The 2026 SPDK Developer Meetup will be in Gdansk, Poland. This is the 6th SPDK Developer Meetup. The meetup will be hosted by Nutanix from Tuesday to Thursday, December 8 to 10, 2026.

SPDK Developer Meetups are a chance to meet other SPDK contributors, discuss new or ongoing feature development, learn about SPDK and build the community.

Meetups are relatively informal but highly technical. Agenda items usually include:

- Introductory presentations on key concepts, especially for new contributors to certain areas of SPDK
- Open discussions and brainstorming sessions on new features or process improvements
- Collaborative whiteboard design work
- Code walkthroughs
- Focused time on getting specific patchsets reviewed and merged

We typically start day 1 with a session on building the agenda for the meetup. If you have specific topics you want to present or want others to present, please reach out to the organizers ahead of time.

Seating is limited, so please RSVP as soon as possible. **Signing up does not guarantee a seat. We will email you a confirmation and further details after you sign up.**
