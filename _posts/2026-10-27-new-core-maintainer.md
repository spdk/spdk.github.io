The SPDK project has a team of core maintainers who are responsible for providing
technical oversight for the SPDK project, including final review and merging of
patches into the SPDK code base. The core maintainer team looks to add to its
team with contributors who are demonstrating technical skill and
leadership within the project over an extended period of time. So we are
pleased to announce that we are adding Boris Glimcher to the core
maintainer team!

Boris has been contributing to SPDK since 2022, with over 400 patches
including a major effort to define the JSON-RPC interface in a
machine-readable schema, which now drives the generated RPC documentation
and keeps the Python CLI consistent with it. He has also been a steady
contributor to SPDK's packaging, CI and test infrastructure, as well as
NVMe/TLS support across the sock layer, NVMe/TCP initiator and nvmf target,
along with its tests. Boris is a Senior Distinguished Engineer in the Office
of the CTO of the Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) at Dell Technologies,
where he focuses on everything storage related, including storage for AI.

Thanks,

Jim Harris (representing the core maintainer team)
